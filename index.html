The Space

Built for the sound in your head

Producer Pmagic is a Nairobi-based recording and production studio built for artists who want their ideas out fast and sounding right. Treated rooms, a producer who listens, and gear chosen for clarity — not clutter.

Every session is one-on-one with the producer, so what you hear in the booth is what you leave with on the final master.
